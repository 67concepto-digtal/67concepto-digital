## 🎯 DESCRIPTOR PARA PLATAFORMAS (inglés — para mejores resultados)

> **COPIA TODO ESTO Y PÉGALO EN LA PLATAFORMA:**

```
Male voice, 35 years old, Latin American Spanish (neutral accent,
no strong regional markers). Professional consultant who enjoys
genuine conversation. Warm but confident tone — like talking to a
trusted advisor who respects your time.

Voice characteristics:
- Pace: Moderate to slow, natural pauses, never rushed
- Tone: Warm, professional, trustworthy
- Energy: Calm confidence, not salesy or aggressive
- Age feel: 32–38, mature but not old
- Accent: Neutral Latin American (intelligible across all Spanish-speaking regions)
- Style: Listens first, then speaks. Like a consultant in a coffee meeting.
- Avoid: High-pitched voices, fast delivery, robotic intonation, overly formal tone

Context of use: KAI is an AI strategic consultant for entrepreneurs and
business owners. Every word should convey expertise, trust, and closeness.
The voice should feel like guidance from a friend who is also an expert.
```

## 🔧 INSTRUCCIONES POR PLATAFORMA

### ElevenLabs (elevenlabs.io)
```
1. Crear cuenta o login
2. Ir a "Voice Lab" → "Create a voice"
3. Seleccionar "Voice Design"
4. Pegar descriptor en "Describe your voice"
5. Pegar TEXTO DE PRUEBA en el editor
6. Generar → Descargar
7. Guardar como: KAI-VOICE-ELEVENLABS.mp3
```

### OpenAI TTS (platform.openai.com)
```
1. Ir a Playground → Audio
2. Seleccionar modelo: tts-1 o tts-1-hd
3. Seleccionar voz más cercana al descriptor (onyx o fable)
4. Pegar TEXTO DE PRUEBA
5. Generar → Descargar
6. Guardar como: KAI-VOICE-OPENAI.mp3

NOTA: OpenAI no acepta descriptor libre.
Usar la voz preestablecida más cercana a la descripción.
```

### Cartesia (cartesia.ai)
```
1. Crear cuenta o login
2. Ir a "Voice Library" o "Clone Voice"
3. Seleccionar "Design voice from description"
4. Pegar descriptor en inglés
5. Pegar TEXTO DE PRUEBA
6. Generar → Descargar
7. Guardar como: KAI-VOICE-CARTESIA.mp3
```

### PlayHT (play.ht)
```
1. Crear cuenta o login
2. Ir a "Voice Lab" → "Create voice"
3. Seleccionar "Design with AI"
4. Pegar descriptor
5. Pegar TEXTO DE PRUEBA
6. Generar → Descargar
7. Guardar como: KAI-VOICE-PLAYHT.mp3
```
